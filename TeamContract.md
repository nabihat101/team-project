## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* Using Discord for communication

* Each teammate agrees to respond to messages in 2 hours on weekdays.

* Group members must notify if missing classes, tutorials, or scheduled meetings with valid reasons. They are responsible for catching up on work asynchronously.

* Respectful and inclusive behaviour are necessary for smooth and productive communication. Some respectful and inclusive behaviours include communicating with each other in labs and classes, actively listening and not interrupting each other, contributing ideas to discussions, and meaningfully contributing.

## Decision Making

* Decisions will be made through majority voting and helping each other understand each perspective

## Conflict resolution

* Listen to each other's perspectives and try to come up with a compromise
* Consult an instructor or TA if resolution has not been made

## Accountability

* Complete work on time and according to schedule
* Seek assistance from teammates/TAs/instructors as needed
